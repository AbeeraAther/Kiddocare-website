About us

A warm little world built around your child

KiddoCare started as one small room and a simple idea — that children do their best growing when they feel safe, known and free to play. Today we care for babies, toddlers and preschoolers in bright, calm spaces with the same familiar faces every day.

Every day is built around real play: messy art, singing, digging in the garden, stories on the carpet, and plenty of cuddles when they're needed.

Our programs

Gentle steps, room to grow

Four programs shaped around what children can actually do at each age — never rushed, always ready for the next thing.

Baby Care

Feeds, naps and cuddles on your baby's own rhythm, with one small team who learn their every cue.

Toddler Care

Busy hands, big feelings and first words — days full of movement, mess and gentle routine.

Creative Learning

Painting, building, pretend play and music — where curiosity leads and skills quietly follow.

Early Development

Sitting, listening, sharing and finishing what you started — the quiet skills that make school easier.

Fun activities

Every day has something to look forward to

A rotating week of hands-on activities that build confidence, language and friendships.

Art & Craft

Paint, glue, glitter and pride — a new making project most days.

Story Time

Books on the carpet, funny voices, and questions we always stop for.

Music & Dance

Shakers, drums and wiggles — rhythm, listening and letting energy out.

Outdoor Play

Digging, climbing, water play and running — every day, rain or shine.
